Dual-Functionality: Vacuum and Blower in One

Unlike traditional handheld vacuums that only offer suction, this device features a dual-ended design. Use the front nozzle to vacuum up crumbs and hair, or flip it around to use the powerful air blower function. This makes it an indispensable tool for cleaning mechanical keyboards, camera lenses, and PC towers where dust hides in hard-to-reach spots.

The Ultimate Car Detailing Companion

Maintaining a clean car interior is easier when you don't have to drag out a heavy shop vac. This mini vacuum is perfectly sized to fit in your glove box or door pocket. It excels at cleaning:

  • Seat crevices and cup holders
  • Dashboard vents and instrument clusters
  • Floor mats and door side pockets

Cordless Convenience & USB-C Charging

Equipped with a rechargeable battery and a universal Type-C charging port, you can power it up using your laptop, a power bank, or your car's USB outlet. The lightweight, ergonomic grip ensures your hand won't tire during longer cleaning sessions around the home or office.

Maintenance and Filter Care

To ensure consistent performance, the device features a washable and reusable filter. Simply twist off the dust container, empty the debris, and rinse the filter under cold water. Ensure the filter is completely dry before reinserting to maintain maximum suction power.

FAQ

How long does the battery last on a full charge?

On average, the device provides 15-25 minutes of continuous use, which is plenty for detailing a car interior or cleaning multiple workstations.

Can the blower function inflate air mattresses?

While it is powerful enough to blow dust and debris, it is designed for cleaning and may not have the high-volume output required for large inflatables.

Is the filter easy to replace?

The filter is designed to be permanent and washable. You only need to replace it if it becomes physically damaged or worn over long-term use.
