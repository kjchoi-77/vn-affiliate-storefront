FAQ

Is the cabinet rust-resistant for kitchen use?

The unit is made of powder-coated carbon steel, which offers good resistance to typical kitchen moisture. However, it is not designed for direct outdoor exposure or constant contact with water.

Can it hold a standard-sized microwave?

Most standard countertop microwaves are too deep for this specific cabinet. We recommend measuring your appliance; this unit is best suited for air fryers, coffee makers, toasters, and spice jars.

Do the doors stay open on their own?

Yes, the doors are designed to slide into a track at the top of each compartment, staying securely out of the way while you access your items.
