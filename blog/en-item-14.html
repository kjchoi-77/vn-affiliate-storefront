Why Switch to a Touchless Motion Sensor Bin?

In a modern home, hygiene and convenience are top priorities. Traditional step-on bins can be clunky, and manual lids are breeding grounds for bacteria. A motion sensor trash can (Code #14) uses infrared technology to detect your hand or knee, opening the lid silently and closing it automatically after a few seconds.

  • Hands-Free Operation: Ideal for when your hands are full while cooking or applying makeup.
  • Odor Control: The tight-sealing lid ensures that unpleasant smells stay trapped inside.
  • Modern Aesthetics: The minimalist design complements contemporary US and EU interior styles, from Scandi-chic to Industrial modern.

Built for High-Moisture Environments

Unlike standard electronic bins, these models are specifically engineered with water-resistant seals to protect the battery compartment and internal circuitry. This makes them the perfect choice for bathrooms or laundry rooms where humidity and occasional splashes are common. While not submersible, they handle the steam from a hot shower or a damp cloth cleaning with ease.

Sizing Guide: Is 14L or 16L Right for You?

Choosing the right capacity is crucial for functionality. The 14L and 16L sizes (approximately 3.7 to 4.2 gallons) are categorized as small-to-medium bins. Here is how to place them:

  • Bathroom & Powder Rooms: The 14L model fits snugly between the toilet and vanity, holding roughly 3-4 days of waste for a standard household.
  • Home Office or Bedroom: The 16L version is excellent for paper waste and light snacks, requiring less frequent emptying.
  • Kitchen: While too small for a primary family kitchen bin, these are excellent as secondary 'organic waste' bins kept on the countertop or under the sink.

Installation and Maintenance Tips

To get the most out of your smart bin, ensure the sensor area is kept clear of obstructions like overhanging cabinets. Most models run on AA batteries or a rechargeable USB module; for the best performance, we recommend using high-quality alkaline batteries to ensure the motor remains responsive for 3-6 months of regular use.

FAQ

What size trash bags should I use for the 14L/16L models?

These bins work best with standard 4-gallon (15-liter) small trash liners available at most US and EU retailers. Look for 'drawstring' versions for easier disposal.

Is the trash can completely waterproof?

It is water-resistant (IPX rated for splashes), meaning it can handle bathroom humidity and light splashes. However, it should never be submerged in water or placed directly under a running shower.

How far away does the sensor detect movement?

The infrared sensor typically activates when your hand or knee is within 6 to 10 inches (15-25cm) of the lid, preventing accidental openings from across the room.
