FAQ

What size trash bags does this bin use?

The 14L and 16L models work best with standard 4-gallon small kitchen or bathroom trash liners available at most local retailers.

Is the sensor too sensitive for tight spaces?

The sensor is optimized for upward motion. However, if placed in a very narrow walkway, we recommend angling the sensor away from the path of travel to prevent accidental triggers.

Can I open the lid manually if the batteries die?

Yes, most models include a manual override or a small notch to lift the lid if power is lost.
