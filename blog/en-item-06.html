Why the Over-the-Sink Design is a Game Changer

In modern US and EU kitchens, counter space is premium real estate. Traditional drying mats often leave puddles and harbor bacteria. This over-the-sink solution ensures that water drips directly into the basin, keeping your countertops bone-dry and hygienic.

  • Vertical Storage: Frees up at least 3-4 square feet of counter space.
  • Natural Drainage: No more stagnant water or messy drip trays to clean.
  • Ergonomic Reach: Keeps frequently used items at eye level for easy access.

Built for Strength: The 60KG (132lb) Load Capacity

Unlike flimsy plastic alternatives, this rack is engineered with high-grade stainless steel. With a tested load capacity of 60kg (approx. 132lbs), it can easily handle cast iron skillets, heavy Dutch ovens, and full sets of stoneware without bowing or wobbling.

Installation & Sizing Tips for Western Sinks

Before purchasing, it is essential to measure your kitchen setup to ensure a perfect fit. This rack is designed for standard single and double-basin sinks common in US and European homes.

  • Faucet Height: Ensure your faucet height provides enough clearance for the lower tier.
  • Cabinet Clearance: Measure the distance between your countertop and the bottom of your upper cabinets; we recommend at least 28-30 inches of total height.
  • Sink Width: Check that the rack's width matches or slightly exceeds your sink's outer dimensions for stability.

Can I customize the layout of the baskets?

Yes, the hanging accessories (utensil holder, knife rack, and soap basket) are modular and can be repositioned along the frame to suit your workflow.
