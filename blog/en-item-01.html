FAQ

Will this backpack fit a 16-inch MacBook Pro?

While designed for standard 15.6-inch laptops, many 16-inch slim-bezel laptops (like the MacBook Pro) will fit comfortably. We recommend checking your device dimensions against the internal sleeve measurements.

How do I clean the PU leather exterior?

Simply wipe the surface with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive brushes to maintain the water-resistant finish.

Is the bag comfortable for long commutes?

Yes, the backpack features adjustable shoulder straps designed to distribute weight evenly, though it is optimized for urban commuting rather than long-distance trekking.
