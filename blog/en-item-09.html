Ergonomic Design: Save Your Back and Knees

Traditional bathroom cleaning often requires uncomfortable kneeling or bending over the tub. This cordless scrubber features a high-torque motor and an adjustable extension pole that reaches up to 45 inches, allowing you to clean floors, baseboards, and high ceiling corners while standing comfortably. It's an essential tool for anyone looking to reduce physical fatigue during weekly chores.

7 Interchangeable Heads for Every Surface

Versatility is the core of the #09 model. The kit includes seven specialized attachments:

  • Large Flat Brush: Ideal for large surface areas like tile floors and floor-to-ceiling windows.
  • Small Flat Brush: Perfect for stovetops, sinks, and soap scum on shower doors.
  • Pointed Corner Brush: Designed for grout lines, corners, and tight faucet bases.
  • Sponge & Cloth Heads: Gentle enough for polishing stainless steel, glass, or even buffing hardwood surfaces.

Cordless Convenience and Power

Powered by a rechargeable battery, this scrubber eliminates the hassle of tangled cords and limited reach. The motor provides consistent rotational speed to lift stubborn stains without the need for aggressive manual pressure. Simply charge via the included USB cable, and you're ready for a deep-cleaning session across multiple rooms.

Installation and Maintenance Tips

Setting up the scrubber is intuitive—the heads snap into place with a simple click. To maximize the lifespan of your device, ensure the charging port cover is securely closed before use. While the brush heads are water-resistant and designed for wet environments, avoid submerging the entire handle or motor unit in deep water.

Yes! The scrubber works exceptionally well with standard bathroom cleaners, degreasers, or simple vinegar solutions. Just apply the cleaner to the surface and let the spinning brush do the agitation.

How long does the battery last on a single charge?

On average, the #09 scrubber provides about 60 to 90 minutes of continuous cleaning time, which is typically enough to deep clean two full bathrooms.

Are the brush heads replaceable?

Yes, the heads are detachable and can be rinsed after use. Replacement sets are widely available to ensure your scrubber stays effective for years.
